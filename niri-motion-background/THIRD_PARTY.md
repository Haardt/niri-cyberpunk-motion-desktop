# Shader attribution

- The original five rosewallpapers shaders: sappyware/rosewallpapers, MIT (root LICENSE).
- `eclipse-corona.frag`: adapted from Subframe's Eclipse Corona, copyright 2026 Subframe, MIT. Source: https://www.subframe.com/motion/eclipse-corona . Full license in `third_party/subframe/LICENSE`. OpenGL 3.3 port, fixed alignment and day/night palette added.
- `god-rays.frag`, `smoke-ring.frag`, `neuro-noise.frag`: adapted from Paper Shaders, Copyright Lost Coast Labs, Inc., Apache-2.0. Source: https://github.com/paper-design/shaders . Original sources, LICENSE and NOTICE preserved in `third_party/paper/`. OpenGL 3.3, procedural hash instead of a noise texture, fewer layers, fixed presets and day/night colors added. Neuro Noise's original algorithm credit to zozuar is retained in the upstream source.
- `black-hole.frag`, `nebula.frag`, `arrakis.frag`, `ocean-sunrise.frag`: original implementations of general space/desert motifs, MIT. These are lightweight 2D illustrations, not copies of the similarly themed Guinetik gallery shaders.

- Additional user-submitted shader ports retain their original notices, including CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 where stated. See `third_party/user-submitted/ATTRIBUTION.md`; the root MIT license does not relicense these assets.

- `portal-turret.frag`: original procedural Portal-inspired sentry turret fan-art, MIT. No original game models, textures or sounds are included.

- `cyberpunk-city-flight.frag`: original procedural neon city, MIT. Geometry, signage and lighting are generated without external textures or audio.

- `cyberpunk-city-rain.frag` and the shared `shaders/lib/cyberpunk-city.glsl`: original procedural city/rain, MIT. Same scene source as the clear variant.

- `aurora-curtains.frag`: original procedural layered aurora curtains, MIT. No external textures or audio.

- `wildflower-meadow.frag`: original procedural windblown wildflower meadow, MIT. No external textures or audio.

- `green-chrome-cubes.frag`: original procedural chrome cube sea, MIT. No external textures or audio.

- `event-horizon-feast.frag`: original artistic black hole with curved light rays and accretion filaments, MIT. No film assets, external textures or audio.

- `bioluminescent-ocean.frag`, `rainy-window.frag`, `emerald-glass-forest.frag`, `ferrofluid.frag`, `autumn-koi-pond.frag`, `infinite-library.frag`, `crystal-cavern.frag`: original procedural scenes, MIT. All geometry, patterns, light and animation are generated without external textures or audio.

- `almost-touching.frag`, `world-execute.frag`, `tangent-garden.frag`, `memory-of-you.frag`, `strange-simulation.frag`: original mathematical and abstract scenes, MIT. No song lyrics, recordings, external textures or audio are included.

- `threshold-of-self.frag`: original fictional consciousness-breakthrough sculpture, MIT. All geometry, light and animation are procedural; no external assets or audio.

- `algebra-of-love.frag`, `shaders/lib/algebra-of-love.glsl`, and `tools/build-algebra-of-love.py`: original shader, original 5x7 bitmap glyphs and deterministic numerical data, MIT. Inspired by the user-described code/mathematics motifs; no video frames, lyrics, recordings or external assets are included.

- `disco-slowmo.frag`: original procedural slow-motion mirror ball, beveled tiles, optical glints, colored shafts and floor projections, MIT. No external assets or audio.

- `retro-boing.frag`: original procedural 1980s demo-scene homage, MIT. Checker-ball geometry, raster colors and CRT treatment are generated without original demo assets, logos, textures or audio.

- `oneiric-silk.frag`: original continuous torus-braid ribbon sculpture, pearlescent shading, reflected folds and light calligraphy, MIT. No external geometry, textures, images or audio.

- `silk-continuum.frag`: original fullscreen folded opalescent environment with gyroid veils, reflections, transmission and depth fog, MIT. All geometry and light are procedural; no external assets or audio.

- `mandelbrot-prism-flight.frag`: original Mandelbrot iteration/flight implementation, precision helpers, chrome-relief lighting and filtered neon shading, MIT. No external shader ports, assets, textures or audio.
